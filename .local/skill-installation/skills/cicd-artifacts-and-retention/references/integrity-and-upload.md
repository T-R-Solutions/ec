# Upload and integrity
Use bounded chunk streaming and compute full-object digest (e.g. SHA-256) across exact bytes. Multipart ETags must not be treated as canonical full-object digests. Verify before publishing immutable manifest. Distinguish blob existence from artifact availability; publish manifest only after verified bytes and authorization checks. A repeated finalize with identical manifest can be idempotent; mismatched digest/size/owner is conflict. Failed staging needs bounded cleanup and diagnostics. An archive may contain traversal, symlinks, hardlinks and bombs; do not automatically unpack untrusted artifacts.
