## Cross-source decisions (engineering synthesis, not requirements)

- **D01:** Expose `ALLOW`, `DENY`, `INDETERMINATE` with source versions to operators; enforce `INDETERMINATE` as no execution. Motivation: O03–O06, O14, A05. The exact Ec response/HTTP status belongs to SDD.
- **D02:** Build separate project, group and role graph evaluators; graph relationships are not interchangeable. Motivation: O01, A01, A03.
- **D03:** Treat deny/permit combining, role inheritance and overrides as explicit **spec choices**, not framework defaults. Cedar and GitLab are deliberately non-authoritative comparisons (O08, O17).
- **D04:** Attach authorization freshness to active desired-state revisions plus operational membership/status/topology versions; prevent stale cached allows on revocation and after sensitive content updates. Motivation: A05, O13–O16.
- **D05:** Separate authorization for changing a role binding from authorization to confer that role's specific capabilities, and validate at commit time. Motivation: A03, O07, O06.
- **D06:** Permission explanation is a **privileged data view**. Show safe provenance and failure freshness without exposing private group membership to unauthorized requesters. Motivation: O04, O19–O22, A05.
- **D07:** Do not suggest alternate declarative config file formats (`.cedar`, `.rego`, OpenFGA JSON). Research supports algorithm/test methodology only; the Ec YAML norm still governs any desired policy configuration.
