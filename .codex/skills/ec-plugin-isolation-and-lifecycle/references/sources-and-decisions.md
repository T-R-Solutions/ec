# Auditable Primary-Source and Scientific Evidence Register

> Imported authoring record. Research dates, consultations and execution claims below belong to the supplied source package; they were not reproduced in this adaptation. Referenced validation scripts, fixtures, product norms and logs are not bundled unless present in this folder. Verify their availability and provenance before use. No historical PASS is current acceptance.

**Research date:** 2026-10-31. **Scope:** Ec plugin artifact identity/integrity, signing and provenance, supply-chain freshness, isolation models, capability grants, runtime limits, lifecycle and truthful administrative UI. Only primary standards, vendor/maintainer documentation, and peer-reviewed venue/journal papers were used. **No forums, Reddit, social posts, benchmarks invented for Ec or unverified claims of real application implementation.** URLs are evidence targets, not automatic proof that the named mechanism was installed.

**Access grading:** `full project norm` = entire supplied local normative source read; `maintainer section` = relevant official online sections read; `paper sections` = accessible original PDF with identified portions/sections examined; `abstract only` = publisher/conference record or extract but not full article review; `publisher excerpts` = partial publisher content. These grades deliberately do not claim exhaustive reading of inaccessible full books or papers.

## Conflicts and evidence limitations

- **Tension resolved:** OCI and Sigstore may carry JSON-formatted transport/attestation data; these are **external evidence/protocol formats**, not additional Ec desired-configuration languages. Ec desired configuration remains YAML only.
- **Tension resolved:** Many plugin systems permit unsandboxed in-process native extensions. This is *not* acceptable as proof of isolation for untrusted Ec plugins; require an actual sandbox and backend authorization gate.
- **Unresolved:** A single blessed artifact runtime, concrete signature trust root, enforcement profiles, capability vocabulary and retention rules cannot be inferred from the existing normative docs. All are explicitly deferred to SDD; execution remains gated.
- Original academic papers were selectively inspected, not universally read end-to-end. P08/P09 are abstract/excerpts only. Academic conclusions apply only within their stated threat models; **no numbers, isolation guarantees or security certification transfer to Ec**.
- No real Ec source tree, signing root, actual plugin artifact, host sandbox, agent, database deployment or browser/AT setup was supplied. The local tests bundled here are synthetic or standalone preflight; **not production security tests**.
